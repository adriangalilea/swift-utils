# MediaSpec marks: provenance

Every artwork in `Marks.xcassets`, where it came from and under what terms.
The catalog is the manifest (`Marks.generated.swift` is derived from it by
`swift run brandgen`); this file records the human side. The reviewed
source set and its manifest are the `/tmp/marks` review of 2026-09-11,
normalised to one ink (`fill="currentColor"`, backgrounds removed) before
import; the flag keeps its colours.

Dolby, DTS, IMAX, Blu-ray Disc, DVD, HDR10+ and Ultra HD are trademarks of
their owners, used nominatively: a chip states that a copy carries the
format the mark names, nothing more.
