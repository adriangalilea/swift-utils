  **The notes GRAMMAR travels with this, and is not the same as a library's.** A library changelog is read on GitHub and nowhere else, so `.github/cliff.toml` here uses the emoji groups every other repo of mine uses. An APP's notes are also rendered by the studio, which DESIGNS releases (a glyph per group) instead of dumping markdown, so an app's notes must be `### Added|Fixed|Changed|Performance|Polish` sections with `- ` items or they render as a dated heading with nothing underneath. Two grammars on purpose, two audiences; do not unify them. Consequence for any app adopting this: ship the app grammar as its `cliff.toml` so `make notes` DRAFTS into the right shape (the seed is what the human edits, so a wrong seed is the actual bug), and gate `make release` on it locally, before anything is signed or published, rather than discovering it when the surface renders blank. awake carries both today (`apps/awake/.github/cliff.toml` + the grep in its `release` target); this pipeline should own them so the next app inherits instead of copying.
